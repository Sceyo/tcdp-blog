Dink n Lob Pickleball is an open-air pickleball venue located at 343 F. Jaca Street, Inayawan, Cebu City. If you’re booking through a ride-hailing app, you can use D’HIVE Arcade as the pinned location for easier navigation. Situated behind Inayawan National High School, the venue is located along a busy street and is accessible to players from the south side of Cebu City.

The venue has a total of three courts, with rates priced at around ₱299 per hour from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and ₱399 per hour from 6:00 PM to 12:00 AM.

From my personal experience, the court flooring feels great, and the ball bounce feels very balanced and consistent during gameplay. One downside, however, is that the courts are fully open, meaning there is no direct shade during rainy weather or extremely sunny conditions. When it rains, players have to leave the court area to find shelter, and during hot afternoons, the sunlight directly hits the court, making it feel very warm.

Despite this, the owners provide excellent service. They offer pickleball balls and paddles for purchase, and paddles can also be rented for only ₱150 for the entire duration of your booking. They even provide free drinking water in a designated corner for players.

Although open plays are occasionally held in this venue, most of the players I noticed were groups of friends and families simply trying to learn and enjoy the sport together. This creates a very welcoming and beginner-friendly environment. One player I met shared that their first pickleball experience in another venue felt intimidating and “not beginner-friendly.” However, when they started booking private games at Dink n Lob, they felt more comfortable learning without feeling judged. I completely understood their perspective, as many people become discouraged when learning something new if the environment feels too competitive or unwelcoming.

For readers interested in trying pickleball, I personally suggest starting with people you are comfortable playing with before immediately joining open plays. While some open plays are beginner-friendly, others may cater more toward experienced players. Having a supportive environment while learning can make a huge difference in your confidence and enjoyment of the sport. Once you feel more comfortable with your skills, that’s the perfect time to explore new venues and join open play sessions with different players.

Overall, Dink n Lob Pickleball is a great venue, especially for players located in the southern part of Cebu City. It’s an excellent place for casual games with friends and family in a relaxed and welcoming environment.

* ## NOTES

* **-** Commuting to the area may be challenging, so using a ride-hailing app is recommended. If possible, it’s easier to go with friends who have a car.
* **-** There are food stalls outside the venue with tables available, but bringing your own snacks and water can help you save money.
* **-** Court bookings can be made through their Facebook page.
