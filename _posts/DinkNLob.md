A player I met at Dink n Lob once told me that their very first pickleball experience felt intimidating — the other players were too competitive, and being a beginner felt unwelcome. So they started booking private courts instead, and that's how they ended up here. The moment they described it, I completely understood. There's a huge difference between learning something new in a forgiving environment and doing it in front of people who've long forgotten what it felt like to be a beginner.

That's what Dink n Lob gets right. And if you're just starting out — or you simply want a casual game with friends or family without the pressure — it's one of the best places to do it in Cebu City.

## Getting there

Dink n Lob is located at 343 F. Jaca Street, Inayawan, Cebu City, situated behind Inayawan National High School along a busy street on the south side of the city. If you're using a ride-hailing app, pin **D'HIVE Arcade** as your destination — it's the most reliable landmark for navigation.

Commuting directly to the venue can be tricky, so if possible, go with friends who have a car. If you're riding solo, a ride-hailing app is your best bet.

---

## The courts

Three open-air courts make up the venue, and the playing experience on them is genuinely enjoyable. The flooring feels solid underfoot, and the ball bounce is consistent — not too lively, not too dead — which matters more than people expect when you're still learning to read the game.

The one honest downside is the open-air setup. There is no overhead shade or shelter directly over the courts. On rainy days, play stops completely and you'll need to find cover outside the court area. On hot afternoons — and Cebu has a lot of those — the sun hits the court directly, and it gets warm fast. It's manageable with the right preparation (bring a towel, stay hydrated), but it's something to factor in when choosing your booking time. Early mornings and evenings are noticeably more comfortable.

---

## Pricing and what's included

The rates are straightforward and among the more affordable in Cebu:

- **₱299 per hour** from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- **₱399 per hour** from 6:00 PM to 12:00 AM
- **Paddle rental: ₱150** for the full duration of your booking

The owners also sell pickleballs and paddles if you're looking to buy your own gear. One small but appreciated detail: free drinking water is available in a designated corner for players. It's a simple touch that makes the experience feel genuinely hospitable.

---

## Atmosphere and who it's for

Most of the players you'll encounter at Dink n Lob aren't there to compete — they're there to have fun. The typical crowd is groups of friends and families trying the sport for the first time or working on their basics together. Open plays are held occasionally, but private bookings dominate the schedule, which keeps the overall energy relaxed.

This makes it an ideal first venue. You won't be walking into a session with seasoned players watching every shot. You'll be playing your own game, at your own pace, without anyone making you feel like you don't belong there yet.

---

## Honest verdict

**What works:** Affordable rates, consistent courts, friendly atmosphere, and genuinely helpful staff. The free water and affordable paddle rental are small extras that add up. It's the right venue for anyone building their confidence in the sport.

**What to watch:** The open-air setup is a real limitation — heat and rain will affect your experience depending on when you book. There's no shade to retreat to mid-session, so plan your schedule accordingly. Early morning slots are the sweet spot.

**Best for:** Beginners, casual players, families, and friend groups who want a low-pressure environment to learn and enjoy pickleball together.

## Book your slot

Bookings are made through their **Facebook page**. Slots can fill up, especially on weekends and evenings, so it's worth reaching out a few days in advance to secure your preferred time.

## Tips before you go

- Use a ride-hailing app and pin **D'HIVE Arcade** for navigation
