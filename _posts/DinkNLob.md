Net and Paddle Pickleball Club is located at 101 Tupas Street, Cebu City. If you’re booking through a ride-hailing app, you can use Ludo & Luym Corporation as the pinned location for easier navigation. This venue is known for having some of the best courts for pickleball in the area. It features around seven courts in total—two championship courts and five regular courts.

For the championship courts, the rate is approximately ₱550 per hour, or ₱650 per hour if you’ll be using the lights. For the regular courts, the rate is ₱500 per hour, or ₱600 per hour with lights. The venue has a more competitive atmosphere, as many players come here for open play sessions or to play DUPR matches and improve their rankings.

So, what is DUPR? DUPR (Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating) is a ranking system where players can input match results from open plays, tournaments, or other games. Other players can also submit match results involving you, which you must verify for authenticity. Based on these matches, the system assigns you a rating. This is especially useful if you want to play competitively and measure your skill level against other rated players. To earn a rating, you typically need to play with someone who already has one—matches between unrated players won’t count. While DUPR isn’t always perfectly accurate due to manual match inputs, some tournaments still require a rating for participation.

The overall environment of Net and Paddle is excellent. Each court is enclosed with cages, helping separate games and minimize interruptions. However, high lobs can still send balls into adjacent courts, which is a normal occurrence. The flooring feels comfortable and slightly cushioned, which reduces bounce compared to cement courts. On the plus side, it’s less rough, making it safer in case of falls.

The players you’ll encounter here range from open play participants and competitive players to families and casual groups. Personally, I enjoy this venue because of its spacious parking area—you won’t have trouble finding a spot. The ventilation is also good, so it doesn’t feel overly hot inside. Additionally, the comfort rooms are well-maintained and even include showers, which is a great feature if you want to freshen up after playing. There’s also a small store that sells pickleballs, water, and snacks.

Overall, Net and Paddle Pickleball Club is a great venue for both casual and competitive play. It offers a balance of a welcoming community and a competitive atmosphere, along with excellent facilities and accessibility.

* ## NOTES

* **-** It’s easier to go with a friend or book through ride-hailing apps. While commuting is possible, routes may vary and are less straightforward.
* **-** You can bring your own water and food to save money, even though there is a store on-site.
* **-** The venue gets booked quickly, so it’s best to reserve your slot as early as possible through Sports360.
