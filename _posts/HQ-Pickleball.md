Walk into HQ Pickleball Cebu on a busy weekend and you'll understand immediately why it's become one of the most talked-about venues in the city. Nine courts running simultaneously, the sharp pop of paddles echoing across painted cement, groups rotating between games — it has the energy of a place where people take the sport seriously but haven't forgotten to enjoy it. I've played here multiple times, with different groups, and that energy has been consistent every single visit.

If you're looking for a venue where you can grow as a player and connect with Cebu's pickleball community, HQ is one of the best places to do it.

## Getting there

HQ is located along Archbishop Reyes Avenue. If you're having trouble finding it, use the **Grand Convention Center of Cebu** as your landmark — the courts are situated in the area where it once stood. 

Parking is ample, making it convenient if you have a personal vehicle. If commuting, jeepney routes **13C** and any route passing **Cabantan** will get you close — disembark at Cabantan and walk the remaining distance to the venue.

---

## The courts

Nine courts total, all with painted cement surfaces. The finish provides solid durability and a predictable bounce, and it holds up well under heavy use. The trade-off is heat — cement absorbs and radiates warmth, and the indoor layout traps it. By mid-session, especially during afternoon bookings, it gets noticeably hot inside.

Practical solution: bring an extra shirt, and consider a small portable fan if you run warm. Morning slots are significantly cooler and worth prioritizing if your schedule allows.

---

## Pricing

Court rental is approximately **₱600 per hour**. Paddle rental is available at **₱350 per hour** if you don't have your own equipment yet. A small on-site store stocks snacks, water, and accessories like grip tape and edge tape — useful if you forget something or want to pick up gear between games.

Seating is available both inside and around the courts, so you can rest, watch ongoing matches, or wait for your slot comfortably.

---

## Open play and the community

Open play is what makes HQ stand out. These are sessions where players of all skill levels join a shared court rotation — you play a game, rotate, meet new people, play again. It's the fastest way to improve and the best way to get plugged into Cebu's pickleball community.

Having played sports my whole life, I can say confidently that the people you play with shape the experience more than the facilities do. At HQ, the mix of competitive drive and genuine friendliness makes sessions consistently enjoyable. It's competitive enough that you'll be challenged, but welcoming enough that you won't feel out of place.

That said, if you're brand new to the sport, consider a few private sessions first to build basic confidence. Not because HQ is unfriendly — it isn't — but because you'll get more out of open play once you understand the fundamentals.

---

## Booking

Reserve your court through their **Facebook page** via the **Sports360** platform. Book at least a week in advance — slots fill quickly, especially on weekends and evenings. Don't wait until the last few days and hope for availability; it's almost never there.

## Honest verdict

**What works:** One of the largest court counts in Cebu, a strong and active community, consistent court quality, accessible location with good parking, and a well-stocked on-site store. The open play culture here is genuinely one of the best in the city.

**What to watch:** The heat is real — afternoon sessions inside can be draining. Bring water and an extra shirt. Booking a week ahead isn't a suggestion, it's a necessity.

**Best for:** Players who want to improve, connect with others, and be part of a growing pickleball community. Also great for groups booking a private court session together.

---

## Tips before you go

- Book at least **one week in advance** — slots go fast
- Morning sessions are cooler and more comfortable than afternoons
- Bring your own water and snacks; the store is convenient but pricier
- Jeepney routes passing **Cabantan** will get you close if you're commuting
- Check their Facebook page for upcoming open play schedules
