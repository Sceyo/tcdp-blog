## Getting there

Net and Paddle is located at 101 Tupas Street, Cebu City. If you're using a ride-hailing app, search for **Ludo & Luym Corporation** as your pinned destination — it's the most reliable landmark for navigation. Parking is spacious and rarely a problem, which is one of those details that matters more than you'd think after a long session.

Commuting is possible but routes can be indirect. A ride-hailing app or going with a friend who has a car is the more practical option.

## The courts

Seven courts in total: two championship courts and five regular courts. All of them are enclosed with individual cage structures, which keeps balls within each court and minimizes interference between games — though a high lob can still sail into the adjacent cage, which is perfectly normal and universally understood.

Out of all the courts I've played in around Cebu, the flooring here stood out the most. It has a texture that feels noticeably softer underfoot compared to the painted cement you find at most other venues — the ball bounce feels more controlled, and your joints thank you after a long session. It's also less rough if you happen to fall, which is something I didn't fully appreciate until I played on it.

Ventilation is one of Net and Paddle's strengths. The airflow inside keeps temperatures manageable even during busy periods, which isn't something you can say about every indoor venue in Cebu.

---

## What is DUPR, and why does it matter here?

DUPR — Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating — is a ranking system where players log match results to earn a skill rating. Results can be submitted by you or by other players who've competed against you, and you verify their accuracy. Once you have a rating, you can compare your level against other rated players and qualify for tournaments that require one.

Net and Paddle is one of the most DUPR-active venues in Cebu. A significant portion of the players here are either building or maintaining their rating, which gives the venue a noticeably competitive but focused atmosphere. To start earning a rating, you'll need to play at least some of your matches against someone who already has one — games between two unrated players don't count toward the system.

If you're interested in playing competitively in Cebu, getting your DUPR rating started at Net and Paddle is one of the most efficient ways to do it.

## The facilities

This is where Net and Paddle genuinely separates itself. The comfort rooms are well-maintained and include **showers** — a feature that almost no other pickleball venue in Cebu offers. If you're playing an intense session and need to head somewhere after, you can actually freshen up before leaving. It's a small thing that signals a venue that has thought carefully about the player experience.

Parking is plentiful and easy to navigate. Seating is available both inside and around the courts for spectators and resting players.

## Atmosphere and who plays here

The crowd at Net and Paddle spans the full range — open play participants, players grinding DUPR matches, families, and casual groups. What ties them together is a general respect for the game. The competitive players aren't dismissive of casual ones, and the casual players aren't intimidated by the level around them. It's a genuinely comfortable place to play regardless of where you are in your pickleball journey.

## Honest verdict

**What works:** The best court quality in this roundup. Cushioned flooring, cage enclosures, championship courts, showers, good ventilation, and a DUPR-active community make this the most complete pickleball experience in Cebu. The parking situation alone is worth mentioning to anyone who's ever circled a block three times looking for a spot.

**What to watch:** Rates are higher than other venues — ₱500 to ₱650 per hour depending on your court and lighting. Slots fill quickly, so booking through Sports360 early is essential. Commuting isn't straightforward.

**Best for:** Competitive players, anyone pursuing a DUPR rating, and players who want a premium facility experience. Also perfectly suited for casual play — the environment welcomes everyone.
