The pickleball community in Cebu has been growing rapidly. Courts can now be found in various locations such as Moalboal, Consolacion, Bogo, Mandaue, Talisay, and in the heart of Cebu City. One of the most popular venues is HQ Pickleball Cebu, located along Archbishop Reyes Avenue. If you’re having trouble finding it, you can use the Grand Convention Center of Cebu as your landmark, as the courts are situated in the area where it once stood.

The term “open play” refers to sessions hosted by pickleball clubs where players of all skill levels can join and play together. This setup helps beginners learn the basics while also giving them the opportunity to meet new people. However, some players may feel intimidated, as not all clubs are beginner-friendly. It’s important to choose open play sessions that match your comfort level.

In my experience, the environment at HQ is both competitive and friendly. I’ve played there with friends and different pickleball groups, and it’s always been enjoyable. What makes a session truly great isn’t just winning—it’s the community you play with. Having played sports for most of my life, I can say that the people you play with significantly affect how fun and engaging the experience is.

As for the facilities, HQ is very accessible, especially if you have a personal vehicle, as it offers ample parking space. Court rental is around ₱600, and there are about nine courts available. There’s also a small store that sells snacks, water, and accessories such as grip tape and edge tape. You can even buy pickleballs or rent a paddle for about ₱350 per hour. Seating is available both inside and around the courts, allowing players to rest or watch ongoing matches.

The courts themselves feel like painted cement, which provides durability without being too harsh on your shoes. However, it can get very hot inside, so it’s best to bring an extra shirt and maybe even a portable fan.

Overall, HQ offers a great combination of community, accessibility, and quality facilities. I highly recommend trying out their open play sessions or booking a court with friends.

* ## NOTES

**-** Book your court at least one week in advance, as slots fill up quickly.
* **-** To save money, bring your own water and snacks.
- **-** If commuting, you can take jeepneys like 13C and stop at Cabantan, or any route that passes by Cabantan, then walk to the venue.
